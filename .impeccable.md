## Design Context

### Users
Chinese-first bilingual readers browsing and reading edited, publish-ready two-person conversations.

### Use Case
Introduce Trash Talk through its existing logo, then let readers find and comfortably read longform conversations on desktop or mobile.

### Brand Personality
Candid, curious, conversational. The project describes itself as a place to pour out thoughts without over-planning the conversation.

### Aesthetic Direction
An independent conversation journal: a minimal, headerless reading surface on pale, softly crumpled paper. Use the existing yellow outlined logo as the exact brand lettering, and the authorized Shoujin typeface for Chinese and English copy. Let the text feel softly printed into uncoated paper, with restrained ink color and ample reading space.

### Design Principles
- Let the conversation and its voices carry the page.
- Keep the homepage quiet, remove navigation chrome, and make the article archive immediately scannable.
- Use the logo's yellow and ink palette with subtle paper grain, without turning the site into a product dashboard.
- Keep longform text readable with a narrow measure, generous leading, and mobile-friendly spacing.
- Prefer semantic HTML, keyboard-visible focus, and reduced-motion support.
